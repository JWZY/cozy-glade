<div class="bestiary-section">
<div class="section-label">Summary</div>
<ul>
<li>A few weeks after the moose stampede, Halden's body is healing, but his pride is still bruised</li>
<li>Bonetop's longhouse is nearly finished; Halden hammers in the final shingle with one of Oleg's bent nail-necklace nails</li>
<li>Oleg hangs the remaining nails as a wind chime while everyone pins up small keepsakes to mark the occasion</li>
<li>Finley calls the longhouse ugly, offers to order linens and banners, then realizes the settlement has no usable address</li>
<li>Finley leaves to start mapping the area, producing a squirrel and deciding he must do everything himself</li>
<li>Oleg plans his nook as "cozy and shiny": blankets, pillows, hoarded forest things, shiny objects, and loosely sorted spell components</li>
<li>Halden's nook is rustic and hearth-adjacent, with live-edge wood, flannels, a carving desk, herbs, inkwells, and his poetry book</li>
<li>Ellery is discovered standing stiffly in Halden's pantry, apparently mistaking it for her closet or charging station</li>
<li>The settlement is running low on metal, especially nails, so Halden suggests attracting a blacksmith with room, board, and rare materials</li>
<li>Oleg proposes communing with the spirits to find precious minerals; Halden offers his longhouse mallet as the ritual gift</li>
<li>On the way to the dragon skeleton, Oleg and Halden catch Vinos whispering practice lines into a sending stone</li>
<li>Vinos admits he has been corresponding with Laureth, an academy acquaintance, and may be falling in love</li>
<li>Oleg and Halden give Vinos advice about bugs, vulnerability, honesty, and not asking Finley for dating help</li>
<li>At Oleg's ritual circle near the dragon skeleton, Halden helps complete the earth runes and feels his magic manifest clearly for the first time</li>
<li>The commune reveals a cave beneath the dragon skeleton where crystalline growths have formed on dragon bones fused into the mountain</li>
<li>A faint tremor follows the vision, but the group decides to press on toward the cave without telling the others</li>
<li>Near the skeleton, the land grows hazy, rotten, and spiritually sick, with plants struggling to grow and then withering</li>
<li>The group finds bats nesting in a dead tree and a weathered stone marker whose residual spirit energy shelters the local wildlife</li>
<li>When they try to chip off a piece of the marker, a giant blue spectral guardian with a war hammer appears</li>
<li>Halden saves the bat tree, shields Oleg from a devastating strike, and nearly drops before Oleg hexes the guardian with Halden's blood</li>
<li>Oleg and Halden join their hand-rune magic in a critical tag-team attack, destroying the guardian and leaving spirit dust behind</li>
<li>The stone marker's protective sanctuary weakens after the guardian falls; Halden heals what he can, but needs rest before entering the cave</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="bestiary-section">
<div class="section-label">Discoveries</div>
<ul>
<li><strong>The Longhouse</strong> — Bonetop's communal home now stands. It is functional, heartfelt, and, according to Finley, aesthetically unforgivable.</li>
<li><strong>Bonetop Has No Address</strong> — Finley cannot order decor or supplies until the settlement knows where it is on the map.</li>
<li><strong>Metal Shortage</strong> — Nails and other worked metal are becoming scarce. A blacksmith would be useful, but the settlement needs materials worth attracting one.</li>
<li><strong>Laureth</strong> — Vinos has been secretly corresponding with a tall, black-haired, wicked-smart academy acquaintance who calls him her "sweet little monkey."</li>
<li><strong>Halden's Magic</strong> — Halden's magic manifests as warm gold-green hearthlight, with small handlike tendrils that seem tied to love, home, and protection.</li>
<li><strong>Dragon-Bone Crystal Cave</strong> — Beneath the dragon skeleton is a cavern where crystals grow over dragon bones fused into the mountain.</li>
<li><strong>Stone Markers</strong> — Primitive, weathered markers line the route toward the cave. Their motif-like carvings hold residual spirit energy and shelter nearby life.</li>
<li><strong>Spirit Dust</strong> — The defeated guardian left behind dust from its dissipated hammer and ectoplasmic form.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="bestiary-section">
<div class="section-label">Threads</div>
<ul>
<li>Finley is mapping the area and may unlock Bonetop's first real address</li>
<li>Vinos wants Laureth to visit once the settlement can tell people where it is</li>
<li>Laureth has asked Vinos about his location, companions, and the size of the encampment</li>
<li>Oleg may add Laureth to his ritual list, though Halden advised against making that part of the courtship pitch</li>
<li>The dragon skeleton's corruption is still spreading through the surrounding land</li>
<li>The cave entrance lies ahead, but Halden is hurt, out of armor, and likely needs rest first</li>
<li>The guardian was territorial rather than clearly evil; destroying it weakened the nearby sanctuary</li>
<li>The spirit dust and dragon-bone crystals may be useful for a blacksmith, Ellery's bracelet, or future spirit work</li>
</ul>
